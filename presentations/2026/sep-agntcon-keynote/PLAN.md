## Design priorities

- Design for delegates at the back of a keynote room, not a laptop reader.
- Big type, big diagrams, big charts. Use the full slide canvas with safe margins.
- No unnecessary tiny labels, footnotes, legends, or decorative text. Put detail
  in speaker notes; simplify or split content rather than shrinking it to fit.
- Voiceover carries the explanation. Each slide makes its main point easy to see.
- Subtle, well-chosen colours, but never at the expense of contrast. Account for
  washed-out projectors, different display technologies, and screen sizes.
- Essential text, chart labels, lines, and states must remain clearly visible;
  colour alone must not carry meaning.

Keep this a rough outline for now, not a detailed slide specification.
